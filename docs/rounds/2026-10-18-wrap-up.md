# The Wrap-up round (Oct 17–18 2026)

**Branch:** `oct18/wrap-up`, over GitHub master `bb39cbb5` (the briefing round, F22 and the Oct 15 small fixes, live since Oct 18 12:58 AM). One commit per phase, each typechecked on its own. Front end only: no rules, index, Cloud Function, cron or Mindbody change, and no new field.

## What AJ asked

The briefing round's handoff (Oct 17) called the Wrap-up "related, not part of" the briefing, and asked that it "fires after the whole session", "captures last observations, confirms what happened today", "surfaces the 'fancy math' (e.g. percent increase on a machine) for small talk", and "shows the next scheduled appointment so the trainer does not need to open Mindbody".

Claude brought a one-page plan (the private page https://claude.ai/artifact/Br2z1S7hJwaABWHXVyRhaC): the real Wrap-up measured on an upright iPad (2,027 px to scroll in a 1,124 px window, 1.8 screens; Effort starting at 1,359 px; 257 words and 30 controls on a quiet session), every card and its taps, five causes, three ways to fix it, three questions, and a working sketch of 1a.

## The phases

1. **Today's machines once, and the labels.** Built (below).
2. **The minute after.** The effort Dial; Note · Sore spot · Pulse · Renewal, each opening under the row, never a dialog; the one note box (N1, N3); a note left unsaved in the session opens the box with its words; the To-file tray asks only about what is still unfiled.

## Phase 1: today's machines once (built)

- **One list.** `WrapUpRow` in `components/WrapUpScreen.tsx`: the machine's name (the display face, a door to its card), today's weight × reps, the change since last time, the gain since the start ("+28% since Mar", in the Now Bar's `--jg-pf-gain`), and next time's weight with − and + beside it. It was three lists (Today; Next session's weights, "Today 154 lb" beside 154; Next time's rows).
- **The gain** is worked out at Finish (`WorkoutTrackerView`, `gainOf`) from the Now Bar's rule, `machine-menu/progress-figure.ts`: today's load against the starting weight on file, or against the first counted set once every session has been read; shown only when up (`rowGainWords`, `lib/post-session.ts`). It counts from the day the starting weight was set (`journey-grid/adapters.ts` now carries `startingWeightDate` as a studio day; until now the grid row never had it, so the journey sentence's "since" always fell back to the first session), or from the first counted session's day.
- **The next weight** is `NextWeightStepper` (`features/next-weight/NextWeightCard.tsx`), the old card's row on today's row: the number's edge turns blue once it differs from today, a tick says it saved, a failed write is said in words, and "Up 4 lb from today. Saved for next time" stays for a screen reader.
- **A machine's name opens its card** on top of the Wrap-up (`onOpenMachine`; the tracker renders the one `MachineMenu` there too, and a note added from it links to the session just finished).
- **How it went** is the one sentence under the list, in the same card. The biggest-gain line, the region chips, Where the work went ("4,760 lb moved") and the three lifetime tiles are gone, and so are the props that fed them (`logs`, `allLogs`).
- **The number** is `thisSessionWords` (`lib/session-total.ts`): the sessions before Journey plus this session's number in Journey ("#416" for a confirmed 412 and Journey's 4th; Mindbody's guess the same way, so the Hub card's "#313" and the Wrap-up's agree). With nothing known before Journey it says no number: the client reads this screen, and "#4" would tell a client of twelve years she is new.
- **Show it, don't label it.** Out: "Wrap-up · session saved" (the eyebrow is "Wrap-up", and says "saved on this iPad", "not saved" or "not finished here" only when that is so), the headline's counts ("6 of 6 machines · 1 max-strength set · load up on 2 · more reps on 2"; the minutes and the number stay), "Today" and "vs last time on each machine", "How it went", "Next session's weights" and its caption, "Next time" over the ticks, "Next" over the booking, and "The session is saved. Anything you add here saves on its own."
- **Next time** keeps its ticks; its Road and the "3 of 6" line left, as they left the briefing (`routine-plan/ui/NextTimeCard.tsx`). `nextTimeRoad` and `nextTimeProgressLine` (`routine-plan/next-time.ts`) are now read only by their tests; the session plan sheet keeps its own Road.
- **Back to Hub** is pinned under the page, outside the scroll, as the briefing's Start is.

Tests: `rowGainWords` and `thisSessionWords` (new cases in `post-session.test.ts`, `session-total.test.ts`); `NextWeightCard.render.test.tsx` mounts the stepper; `WrapUpScreen.render.test.tsx` holds the new order (the list with its sentence, effort, the booking, Back pinned outside the scroll), the rows (gain only when up, the star, no stepper on a machine not performed, the name's door), the eyebrow, the number, and no Road.

## Phase 2: the minute after (built)

- **One card, in the order the trainer acts:** the effort Dial, open, then one row, **Note · Sore spot · Pulse · Renewal**, each opening under the row, one at a time (`AfterChip`, `components/WrapUpScreen.tsx`). The card's head ("Effort · profile note · Pulse"), "Effort", "The whole workout, judged by you", "Left untouched, it saves As expected." and the effort's "Saved" tick went: the word the Dial rests on is its answer, and only a failed save says more ("Not saved. Tap it again."). Untouched, it still saves As expected on the way out.
- **Note is the note box** (`features/client-notes/NoteBox.tsx`, `note-box.ts`; the notes README says it in full): Goes to (Profile only · Next time · Critical, and Just for me), What it's for (the five categories), and the line saying where it lands, all before a word is typed. It starts at Profile only; a category moves it to its own default until the trainer places it. "Matters until" is not on this box: Next time is the next four briefings and Critical stays until closed, and a date is still a tap away on the profile's composer.
- **N1 a, everywhere a note is written:** the one Loudness control says Profile only · Next time · Critical under "Goes to" (the Wrap-up, the profile's composer and its summary chip, the machine card's notes). Stored values and a note's badge keep their names.
- **A note the session left unsaved** opens the box with its words, its category and where it was going, and Note open. Handed over, it keeps the draft's machine, flavour and body parts (`fromDraft`), and the draft is never filed on its own as well, even when its words were cleared. A note for the studio's floor notes on a machine keeps its own offer (Add to Westlake's notes · Drop it): it never goes on the client's record.
- **Sore spot (N3 a):** the briefing's figure and its rating step (`BodyModel`, `BodyStateTracker`, `briefing/stack.ts`); each rated spot is filed on the way out as a Health note with its spot, at Next time ("Knees · Stiff", its "matters until" day when one was set). The line under it says where it lands. No new field.
- **Pulse opens in place** (`PulseQuickLog`, the note sheet's content): an area, a Dial, Done. It was a dialog over the screen.
- **Renewal** keeps its dialog: the conversation is a whole form (a leaning, concerns, plans, a note). Its chip is loud only when the conversation is due.
- **Filing** (`WorkoutTrackerView`, `filePostSessionNotes`): the box's words where it said, at its loudness, under its category (unfiled when none); Just for me to the trainer's own notebook (`jotAbout`); each spot a Health note. Every write issued side by side, each saying its own refusal; every way out files once, as before.

Tests: `note-box.test.ts`; `WrapUpScreen.render.test.tsx` (the box before a word is typed, a category's default and a placed note, Just for me, the draft opening the box and handed over as the draft's, Pulse in place, a sore spot rated and handed over, the chips' look); `WorkoutTrackerView.render.test.tsx` (the note filed under its category, a spot filed as "Knees · Stiff" at Next time); the Loudness words in `Dial.render.test.tsx`, `MenuNotes.render.test.tsx` and `notes.render.test.tsx`.
