AJ then talked it through in a claude.ai conversation and brought its handoff, Oct 17: **`2026-10-17-briefing-handoff.md`** beside this file, kept word for word. "So let's go ahead and do it."

The handoff builds the briefing from three questions, in this order: **What's different about this person?** · **What are we doing today?** · **How have they been doing?** Its trust rule: "Start smaller than feels right. A briefing that is noisy or wrong once will be ignored."

## The handoff against the code (checked Oct 17)

- **Already true, no work:** a cancelled B makes the next session B (`routines/next-routine.ts` alternates off the last finished session); a hidden note comes back when it is updated, and closed threads stay off (`client-notes/dismissals`, the thread zones); a Heads up goes by sessions, not days (four, `client-notes/heads-up.ts`).
- **Differs from the code:** Loudness is three levels, not binary (Note · Heads up · Critical, `rating/Loudness.tsx`); "just for me" notes don't exist (only the private "No need to remind me"); performance history appears twice in the handoff (a box under question 1, and question 3), so it is one place: question 3.
- **How it maps onto the plan page:** question 2's controls (swap, add, rebuild, any routine) are the page's **1a, Today's list**, plus Rebuild and Other routine; question 1 brings FORD follow-ups, the page's **2a**; the Wrap-up is "related, not part of", so it comes after.

## The phases

1. **What are we doing today?** Today's machines as one list. **Built** (below).
2. **What's different about this person?** Safety, Heads up, the FORD line with a follow-up first (F13), the capture (Note, Sore spot with a head, Dials, Pulse), F4's X back to where Start was tapped, "Last session · Routine A". F20's "no number until the Dial is touched" in the Pulse's pain map.
3. **How have they been doing?** Today's machines, their last 5 to 10 sessions as plain numbers, a placeholder until the visual has its own round; "View all machines" folded.

Then AJ walks it in Demo Mode on the iPad.
