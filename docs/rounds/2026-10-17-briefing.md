## What AJ asked

On the three-day review, Oct 16 2026, after ten screenshots of Robbin McNeill's briefing at Solon: "There's a lot of information that's kind of spread out all over, and there's a lot of stuff to click on. I mean, just let alone to add one machine onto the pre-session briefing is like five or six clicks. It needs to be a little bit quicker than that, maybe two at max, three. But definitely not more than four."

Claude brought a one-page plan (the private page https://claude.ai/artifact/67kmxTbDkqDBkajujSu678: every card and its taps, five causes, three directions, three questions). AJ then talked it through in a claude.ai conversation and brought its handoff, Oct 17: **`2026-10-17-briefing-handoff.md`** beside this file, kept word for word. "So let's go ahead and do it."

The handoff builds the briefing from three questions, in this order: **What's different about this person?** · **What are we doing today?** · **How have they been doing?** Its trust rule: "Start smaller than feels right. A briefing that is noisy or wrong once will be ignored."

## The handoff against the code (checked Oct 17)

- **Already true, no work:** a cancelled B makes the next session B (`routines/next-routine.ts` alternates off the last finished session); a hidden note comes back when it is updated, and closed threads stay off (`client-notes/dismissals`, the thread zones); a Heads up goes by sessions, not days (four, `client-notes/heads-up.ts`).
- **Differs from the code:** Loudness is three levels, not binary (Note · Heads up · Critical, `rating/Loudness.tsx`); "just for me" notes don't exist (only the private "No need to remind me"); performance history appears twice in the handoff (a box under question 1, and question 3), so it is one place: question 3.
- **How it maps onto the plan page:** question 2's controls (swap, add, rebuild, any routine) are the page's **1a, Today's list**, plus Rebuild and Other routine; question 1 brings FORD follow-ups, the page's **2a**; the Wrap-up is "related, not part of", so it comes after.

## The phases

1. **What are we doing today?** Today's machines as one list. **Built** (below).
2. **What's different about this person?** Safety, Heads up, the FORD line with a follow-up first (F13), the capture (Note, Sore spot with a head, Dials, Pulse), F4's X back to where Start was tapped, "Last session · Routine A". F20's "no number until the Dial is touched" in the Pulse's pain map.
3. **How have they been doing?** Today's machines, their last 5 to 10 sessions as plain numbers, a placeholder until the visual has its own round; "View all machines" folded.

Then AJ walks it in Demo Mode on the iPad.

## Phase 2: what's different about this person (built)

**2a, what the briefing says** (the briefing's README, "What's different about this person", says it in full):

- One card for what's new: Since last time, the Heads ups, the team's focuses, the FORD line, and the InBody and renewal lines at its foot (it was four cards). No count beside the label.
- **Quieter once seen** (AJ's "2a"): a Heads up is a full card until this trainer has trained the client since its newest word, then one quiet line. Critical never goes quiet.
- **The FORD line by the Hub's rule** (F13, read as the page's 2a): a day coming up this week, then a Follow up next time question, then one that just happened, then news, in the Hub's own words. Saving the answer clears the question; "Nothing new, clear the question" clears it without one. No new mark for big life events: a FORD detail with its date already marks the Hub card for the whole studio.
- **No "Mind the limits on …"** under the routine or the plan card (AJ's "3a").
- **Back where Start was tapped** (F4 "a"): "Back to the Hub", "Back to Calendar", "Back to Clients", "Back to Robbin's profile".
- The last session names its routine, never "Standard".

**2b, what a trainer fills in:**

- No heading over the chips; four of them: Dials · Sore spot · Note · **Pulse** (You ask, or Hand over the iPad).
- **A sore spot is saved by its Dial tap**: Sore spot → the body → how it is, three taps (was four, five from the list). A "Matters until" day picked after is kept; Done closes.
- **Head** is a spot (AJ's F20 note, "head ache"): tapped on the figure or picked from the list, lit on the figure, recorded like any spot, and read by nothing that changes a machine (whether a headache from exercise should flag one is head office's open injury-map question). The codex's Body figure doesn't pin it yet (parking list). "Under the weather" stays on "How are you feeling?".

**2c, F20 "a" in the Pulse** (opened from the briefing's Pulse chip, and from the profile): a pain spot tapped on the map has no number until its Dial is touched. It shows as "Not rated", is never compared, ranked the worst or flagged, and the pain lane reads it as unknown. The Pulse's README, "Not rated until touched".
