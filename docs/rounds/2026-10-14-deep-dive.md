# The Deep Dive for a client older than Journey (Oct 14 2026)

Branch `oct14/deep-dive`, from master `55a9e005`. Not pushed. Front end only: no rules, index, Cloud Function, Mindbody or job change.

## What AJ asked

After the FileMaker trial import, AJ named the Deep Dive next to the progress report ("i want to use the data we have to work on some parts of the app like the client progress report and the deep dive"). The Deep Dive (`src/features/clinical-review/`) was already in the app's own look and readable on an iPad; what went wrong was what it SAID about a long-standing client, whose history is now mostly an import: weight and reps, and nothing else the floor records today.

Drawn for a made-up client with 199 FileMaker visits and then 21 in Journey (`harness/deep-dive`), it said:

- "220 of 220 sessions raised nothing. Nothing hurt", about 199 visits that never had a pain record;
- "Dials tapped 10%" and "time under tension recorded on 10% of sets", when every Journey session was asked and every Journey set was timed; the time under tension chart was hidden ("not recorded on enough sets yet") and a finding said the same;
- "Worth a deliberate progression, a cadence change or a different machine for the same target" on every stall, which the floor's rule forbids ("Never a suggestion to move the weight");
- the top five findings were the five stalls listed again in the very next section;
- "Morning (be…" and "Evening (aft…".

AJ's answers, Oct 14 2026: **"Fix what it says (Recommended)"**, **"Worth asking why (Recommended)"**, **"One line for all stalls (Recommended)"**.

## What was built

**Deep Dive 1** (`115508a7`):

- A session recorded before Journey is marked on its fact (`beforeJourney`, by client-history's `isLegacySession`: the FileMaker import's `legacy_filemaker_id`, the chart importer's "Legacy" or "Chart" trainer). What only Journey records is counted over the sessions Journey recorded: time under tension's share of sets, the Dial's share of sessions, the two coverage findings ("in Journey"), and pain ("21 of 21 sessions Journey recorded raised nothing. The 199 from before Journey carry no pain record.").
- One line under the caveat, only when the range reaches back before Journey: "199 of these 220 sessions are from before Journey: weight and reps only. Rep quality, time under tension, the Dial and pain count from Jul 17, over the 21 sessions Journey recorded."
- The time under tension chart starts at the first session Journey recorded, weekly while that is 26 weeks or fewer (`report.tutWeeks`, `tutPeriod`), not two years of empty axis.
- The stalls are one finding, "5 machines at the same weight for 10 sessions or more", each one named, with a button down to Progression stalls; a single stall keeps its own card. A stall and a load that fell end "Worth asking why." A stall is still decided by the load alone (AJ, Oct 2 2026).
- A level's words wrap; a "Log past session" placeholder start (12:00 UTC, 8 AM in Ohio) is not read as a time of day.

**Deep Dive 2** (`cd2b798e`): the preview, `harness/deep-dive`: the app's own `buildReport` and `ClinicalDashboard` from a made-up client shaped like the import, photographed at an iPad held upright, light and dark.

```bash
node harness/deep-dive/shots.mjs all 90d
```

The pictures go to `%TEMP%\journey-deep-dive\`, the whole page and each screen's worth.
