## 2. What the audit found (short)

- **The notice with no door was in two places.** Operations → Today listed an
  open session (one started before "Who's this?" was answered) as "A client ·
  Left open" with no Open button, and Admins → Home's "Sessions left open"
  led to a studio page that lists no sessions. Only the trainer who started an
  open session could get back to it, and only for about a day.
- **Nothing ends a session by itself.** It ends when someone taps Finish,
  Discard, or "Finish it as it was". A paused session, or one sitting at End
  Session, read "Left open" on every other iPad after an hour.
- **The same session could be finished, and counted, twice:** a session taken
  over from an iPad that was offline, one trainer signed in on two iPads, two
  people choosing "Finish it as it was". The client's totals counted twice,
  the late iPad's older weights went over the newer ones, and the trainer
  credit flipped back.
- **Mindbody and Journey don't double count** sessions left (that is
  Mindbody's number alone). But: an unfinished session counted as a visit in
  the renewal pace, the last visit and the Journey state; and a client whose
  first Journey session came from Log past session got a lifetime total that
  counted Journey's sessions twice (#320 for 310 real ones).
- **The session number** was given at Start and written back at Finish, so a
  left-open session and a new one could share a number.
- **Switching studio is per iPad** and never moves anyone else's screen.
  Trainers finishing different clients at the same moment is safe.
- **At scale,** the first things to give are Mindbody staff numbers (now: its
  own round), announcements at about 12 studios and the trainer list at about
  30. Those are later rounds.

## 3. What was built

### Part C: a finish is counted once, and Continue on this iPad

- **The marker.** Finish's first write (the session batch: the session to
  Completed, every set, every machine setting) now also creates one small
  document, `sessions/{id}/finish/once`. The database allows it only when that
  same write moves the session from not finished to finished. The second write
  (the totals batch: the client's counters and machine totals) marks it
  counted, once, by the same person and the same finish. A second finish of
  the same session from the new app is refused whole: no second count, no
  older sets over newer ones, no credit flipped back, no older weights.
- **Never block a save.** A first finish always passes. When a finish is
  refused, the iPad asks the server what happened (a bounded read that never
  mistakes "no answer" for "no marker") and says so in plain words. If the
  refusal wasn't the marker at all (the rules not deployed, a rule bug), it
  finishes the old way, once.
- **The device holding the session** (`finishFromHolder`, the attack's fix).
  Each iPad keeps an id; a session carries `recordingDeviceId`, written at
  Start, Resume, a take-over and Continue. The marker is accepted only from
  the device the session names, from a session no device holds (every session
  from before this round), from the trainer's own sign-in on a device that
  can't keep an id, or once nothing has been saved in it for an hour. So
  after a take-over, the iPad it was taken from can't finish first and put its
  credit and older sets back.
- **"Finish it as it was" is not the last word.** When the iPad that ran the
  session comes back online with its own Finish, its sets, weights and note go
  in over the as-it-was copy, with no second count.
- **A finished session keeps who holds it** (`finishedHolderKept`): once a
  session is Completed, nothing may change its trainer, its starter, its
  device or its heartbeat.
- **Sets and settings by reach** (`logSessionReach`, `machineSettingsClientOk`,
  the attack's fix): a set is written only by someone who can reach its
  session, and a client's machine settings only by someone who can reach the
  client. Before, any signed-in trainer at any studio could blank, rewrite or
  delete another studio's sets and next weights.
- **The session's number** is added at Finish (`sessionCount: increment(1)`),
  never set to the number shown at Start (audit #8).
- **Continue on this iPad.** A trainer's own live session that another device
  is recording opens as watching, with Continue on this iPad.
- **Clearing a session left open, by anyone at the studio.** The stale
  question asks Resume it, Finish it as it was, Discard it, and Start a new
  session (your own) or Not now (someone else's). Discard asks twice, checks
  the server first for someone else's session, and is one write with every set.
  Someone else's session left open **today** can be resumed or finished as it
  was, and discarded only from the next studio day (a Finish queued on an
  offline iPad would otherwise be lost).

### Part A: the doors and the labels

One "Open" on every unfinished session, wherever a leader or a trainer looks:
Operations → Today, Hours, Admins → Home (to that studio's Today), the
client's profile, the Client Directory and the Hub. A session that went quiet
says why: **Paused**, **At End Session** or **Left open** (`lib/session-quiet.ts`;
Paused and At End Session only on their own day). Only "Left open" counts as
something that needs a leader. A session left at another studio is described
("Left open at Solon"), never cleared from here.

### Part B: the counting

- **Only finished sessions are visits** (`attendanceFromSessions`): the
  renewal pace, the last visit, the Journey state (the nightly renewals job)
  and the profile's live renewal.
- **The lifetime total's double count** (`visitsBeforeJourney`): Journey's
  own sessions come off Mindbody's visit count whenever Journey has any, not
  only once a first day was recorded. 310 visits and 10 Journey sessions is
  #310 again, not #320.
- **The sum beside Confirm** on Account, so a trainer sees the arithmetic
  before confirming.

## 4. The rules, and what they do to iPads still on the old version

iPads load a new version only on the Hub, so for a while old and new run side
by side. The rules go before the app (the release's deploy order).

**Added (only the new app uses them):**

- `match /finish/{docId}` with `finishOnceOk`, `finishFromHolder` and
  `countedOnceOk`: the marker. The sessions' own rules do NOT require it, so
  every Finish from an old-version iPad goes through exactly as today.
- `recordingDeviceId` is a plain session field the existing rules already
  allow.

**Removing access, by name** (the attack's fixes, beyond the plan):

1. **`logSessionReach`** on `exerciseLogs` (create, update, delete): a set is
   written only by someone who can reach its session (its trainer, anyone who
   works at or leads its studio, anyone who can read its client).
2. **`machineSettingsClientOk`** on `clientMachineSettings` (create, update,
   delete): only someone who can reach the client, and an update may not move
   a document to another client.

And a third, narrower one: **`finishedHolderKept`** on `sessions` update: a
Completed session's `trainerId`, `trainerName`, `trainerInitials`,
`startedByTrainerId`, `recordingDeviceId` and `lastHeartbeatAt` can't change.

**Why that is safe for the old version** (checked against the live app,
`5a0bf7ea`): the old app writes sets only into sessions its trainer can reach,
and settings only for the client on screen, which it could open only because
it can read that client; every settings writer names its client. A set with
no session, or whose session doesn't exist yet (a Start or Log past session
batch) or any more (a discard's sweep), still passes, and so do History's
Delete and every Discard (rules read the state before the batch). The old app
never changes a Completed session's trainer or heartbeat: take-over, Resume
and the heartbeat are written only on a session it shows as running, and
History's edits, the Wrap-up's effort and the Demo Mode seed touch none of
those fields. The one old-version write now refused is the one the rule is
for: a heartbeat or take-over from a late iPad onto a session another iPad
already finished (the old app reports it as a write that failed; nothing it
holds is lost, because the session is already finished). The rules tests hold
each case (#22, A1 to A5).

**One new index:** `sessions (hostedAtStudioId, status, createdAt desc)`, for
Hours' "Not finished yet".

## 5. Known limits

1. **A colleague's Discard on a later day can lose an offline iPad's queued
   Finish** (rules test A4, recorded as it stands). The rules can't tell a
   Finish arriving after a discard from a second finish. The doors carry it:
   someone else's session left open today is discarded only from the next
   studio day, and today it can be resumed or finished as it was, which a late
   real finish still lands over. An iPad offline across a whole day with an
   unsent Finish is the gap.
2. **An iPad still on the old version can still count a session twice** (it
   writes no marker). It can no longer flip the credit (`finishedHolderKept`).
   The gap closes as each iPad loads the new version on the Hub.
3. **A late iPad's set-by-set writes still land.** Sets are written one by one
   as they are typed, outside Finish, so a late offline iPad can still put an
   older number on a set the winner changed, and a losing iPad whose session
   was discarded can recreate orphan sets. The counts and the session are
   protected; single sets are not.
4. **Markers outlive deleted sessions** (History's Delete). Harmless, since
   session ids are never reused; the pre-launch reset sweeps them
   (`scripts/reset-test-data.ts`, commit `f67b9813`).
5. **The rules reach further than the doors** (test #24): a trainer at the
   client's home studio, or at an approved cross-train studio, can still take
   over or delete an unfinished session hosted elsewhere at the rules level.
   The doors refuse it. Tightening it removes access, so it waits for AJ.
6. **A leader who runs the host studio but can't read a cross-train client
   can't finish its session** (test A5). It is never half counted; widening it
   belongs with the privacy lock's client read rule.
7. **Paused and At End Session are words, not a heartbeat.** After an hour
   with nothing saved, a paused session is open to anyone at the studio to
   clear; the Discard check, the "last on it" line and the next-day wait are
   what keep a colleague from clearing it carelessly.
8. **A resend whose marker read fails** is reported as refused although it
   landed (the profile then shows it finished): a false alarm, never a lost
   save or a double count.
9. **Clients who already confirmed an inflated "before Journey" number** stay
   wrong: the confirmed record outranks the guess. Listing them needs a
   production read, a follow-up for AJ.
10. **Rare duplicate notes** from a losing iPad's queued Finish (the note for
    the next trainer and pain notes are filed outside the batch).
