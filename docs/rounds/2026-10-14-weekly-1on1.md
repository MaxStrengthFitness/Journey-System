## 4. What the Gemini design review taught

AJ brought a design review written by Google's Gemini, "Next-Gen UI and
Catalog Design" (Oct 14 2026). **It read the roadmap, not the code.** It
proposed building a studio map that was already live and "upgrading" setting
tiles that were already typed. About half its ideas were built already or went against a decision AJ had
made. Four were worth keeping (Q10).

**The lesson:** an outside reviewer is only as good as the documents it reads,
so the documents have to stay current. That is Q8, and the reason this pass
exists and is repeated before every 1-on-1.
