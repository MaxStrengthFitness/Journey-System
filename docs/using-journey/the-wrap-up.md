# After the session: the Wrap-up

The Wrap-up opens after you tap **Finish session**, while you walk the client out. It's one page, top to bottom in the order you use it.

**Today's machines** lists each machine once: the weight and reps, the change since last time, and the gain since the client started. On each row you set **next time's weight** with − and +. Tap a machine's name to open its card.

**How it went** is one sentence under the list.

**Effort** is the Dial, from "Left some in the tank" to "Gave everything". If you don't touch it, it saves "As expected".

**The chips** are **Note**, **Sore spot**, **Pulse** and **Renewal**.

- A note asks where it goes: Profile only, Next time or Critical, or Just for me. Then what it's for. It tells you where it will land.
- A sore spot after the session is filed as a Health note. Pain or stiffness goes to Next time, so the next trainer sees it.
- The note you wrote at Finish waits here to be filed. It stays on the next briefing either way.

**Next session** shows the client's next booking. If nothing is booked in the next 30 days, it says so: book the next one before they leave. **Times with room** shows open times you can offer. Journey doesn't book, so check it in Mindbody before you promise it.

**Back to Hub** is at the bottom when you're done.

The Wrap-up never picks next time's weight for you.
