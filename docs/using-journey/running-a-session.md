# Running a session

**Start.** Tap **Start session** on the client's Hub card, their profile, or the bottom bar. The briefing opens first, and its **Start session** bar begins the session.

**The bar at the top** shows the time, how many machines are logged, anything to know before you touch a machine, **Notes**, **Pulse** and **Finish**.

**The Now Bar at the bottom** is the machine in your hand:

- **Set up** opens the machine's card on the first setting that's missing. Once set, it shows the settings.
- The weight from last time is already in. Change it with − and +, or type it.
- Last time's reps show in grey. Today's reps are never filled in for you: type them. Tap **Next** with nothing typed and nothing is logged.
- Pick the form: **Needs improvement**, **Completed** or **Max strength**.
- **No set?** gives Practice, Blood flow or Skip. Skip asks why.
- On the last machine, Next becomes **Add another machine**. Tap + beside any machine on the floor list to add it.

**Finish** is at the top, away from your thumb. It asks if you're done and has a box for a note to the next trainer. **Scrap session** underneath throws the session away. Finish never hangs and never counts a session twice.

**A walk-in, or Mindbody is down?** Client, then **Open session**. Run it as usual and tap **Who's this?** at any time, or at Finish. Settings you type are kept and go to the client when you choose them.

**The Wi-Fi drops?** Keep logging. Everything is saved on the iPad and sends when the connection is back. Don't close or reload Journey while it's offline.

Journey never picks the weight or tells you when to go up. That's yours.
