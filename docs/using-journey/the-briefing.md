# Before the session: the briefing

The briefing opens when you tap **Start session**, just before the client sits down. It's everything you need, in the order you need it. Nothing on it stops you starting, and nothing on it is private, because the client may look.

**Before you start** is safety only. The body figure lights up the client's limits, with any Critical notes beside it. When there's nothing, it says "Nothing flagged — clear to go."

**Since last time** is how the last session went, the notes left for this one, what you're focusing on, and something to ask about from their life outside the studio. A quiet line tells you when an InBody scan is due.

**The chips** under it are for anything you want to add now: the Dials, a Sore spot, a Note, or Pulse. A note has a **Just for me** switch, for your own notebook.

**Today's routine and today's list.** The client's routine is laid out as today's machines. **Swap** one, take one off, **Add a machine**, change the order or rebuild the list. Small warnings show when the order goes against the Academy's.

**How have they been doing?** is folded away. Open it for the last few sessions on today's machines, as plain numbers.

When you're ready, tap **Start session** at the bottom.

The briefing is only for before the session. Everything after Finish is on the Wrap-up.
