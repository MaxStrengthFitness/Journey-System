# Finding your way around

**Getting in.** Open Journey from the J icon on the iPad's Home Screen and sign in as yourself: Google, or Microsoft with your @maxstrengthfitness.com address. The studio is pinned once per iPad, so you're greeted with it.

**The bar along the bottom** is how you move around:

- **Hub**: today's bookings, a column for each trainer. Tap a card for its peek, with **Open profile** and **Start session**. A red triangle means the client has a Critical note. **Key** explains every mark on a card.
- **Client**: the client directory. Search by name, or switch between All, My clients, Kaizen and In today.
- **Start session**: while a session runs it shows the client's name, and a tap takes you back to it.
- **Learning**: the machines and the Academy, with one search.
- **My Studio**: Relay (the team's board, your own list, the journal), Openings, Machines and the studio's details.
- **Calendar**: the schedule by month, week or day.

**A client's profile** has four tabs, from a quick look on the floor down to the whole record: **Journey**, **Programming**, **Notes & Profile** and **Activity Archive**.

**Your own page**: tap your initials, top right, then View profile. Your week, the clients you train most, and your usual week are there.

**Another studio**: tap the studio's name at the top. Switching lets go of the client you had open. A session that's running keeps running.

**Handing the iPad over**: tap **Not you? Sign out**, so the next person starts clean. If your session is still open, it asks first.
