## Product

### Corporate owns the method

**Corporate owns the method, so corporate reads it before adopting it** (the catalog gate, Sep 20). A studio's own machine carries a whole definition, method included; publishing one makes those words Max Strength's on every floor. An admin reads the machine in the catalog editor and may rewrite it first, and what they saved is what publishes. Publishing is refused while the machine is missing anything the app would render wrong or the floor would coach wrong — the one place this app holds a write, and it is the opposite case from "never block a save", which protects a trainer mid-session.

### Operations says it once

**Operations says it once, and says less** (AJ, Oct 3 2026: "there's just so many words on there. It's really overwhelming"; "i trust all your recommended"). A page leads with one line of counts, never a written bottom line; what it can't judge yet (the nightly record) is ONE note at the top, never repeated per section and never called clear; a row is one line, its how-to and source on its (i); no captions under headings; an empty section is a name in one "All clear" line. A count is not a score: the proof stays one tap away. The pieces are in `src/features/admin/README.md` → Voice.

**What Operations is for** (AJ, Oct 15 2026, a comment on the CLAUDE.md trim's review page): "studio leaders are extremely busy. handling retention, client concerns, contracts issues, ontop of everything else a local business will do like attend town bussiness meetings and handle new clients , handle trainers and also even train clients amongst many other things so the operations dashboard needs to be able to run a studio leader through everything going on in a brief way but allowing them to dive deeper if they want to investigate, its the briefing where they are deciding what to do next. leaders can be juggling lots of tasks at a time and sometimes have to let issues sit before they can be solved so we need to be clear but concise ... Its pretty much a main briefing dashboard with the ability to investigate and dive deeper and be warned of issues before they happen". So every Operations page is judged by four things: brief first, deeper one tap away, an issue can be left to sit without being lost, and what is coming is said before it arrives.

### Sessions left

**Sessions left means left in the contract; given sessions are extra, beside it** (AJ, Sep 26 2026: "How many left in their contract is the correct number, but ... clients can be given sessions that also plays a part, so a left in contract and then extra sessions works"). "36 left in contract · +12 extra": `sessionsSplit` in `client-admin/account.ts`, the studio's package table first. A contract paid a month at a time says "on hand", never left, until the renewal counts the payments to come. **Sessions left and sessions recorded are different numbers and are never mixed** (AJ, Oct 2 2026: "A client could have 42 sessions in their package but only have 40 sessions by the end of their package due to late cancels"): left is the contract's, from Mindbody; recorded is visits. **Late cancels** are a mark anyone at the studio makes on a booking nobody logged ("Late cancel · session taken", the Hub's peek and Operations → Today; `studios/{s}/bookingMarks`, `noShow: true`, `src/lib/late-cancels.ts`), a leader changes or undoes one ("At the end of the day it is up to the studio and its leaders to make the final call"), and they are tallied beside visits ("40 sessions · 2 late cancels"), never inside them; the renewal pace counts one as a session used, never as a visit. **Given sessions count toward the renewal** (AJ, Oct 2 2026: "Yes, count them in"): the conversation waits until they are used too.

### Every number has a reader

**Every number has a reader.** A field we write must be read by at least one screen, report or job; a write with no reader is a bug to fix, not a field to delete (`docs/business/data-and-metrics.md`). The session's `clientAge` / `clientOccupation` / `clientIsRetired` / `clientActivityLevel` / `clientClinicalProfile` snapshot is deliberate — it is the substrate for cohort analytics (starting weights, which machines suit whom, pace, the studio's own claims) and it is a SNAPSHOT because those facts change. Any cohort metric keeps the existing rules: a named minimum sample, cells k-anonymous at five (`CELL_MIN_CLIENTS`), no clinical data in anything a signed-in trainer can read per-person, and recognition never ranking.

**Why we collect it** (AJ, Oct 15 2026, a comment on the CLAUDE.md trim's review page): "we live in a era of data, anything we collect we want to use and try to use it in the most informative way to allow trainers to go above in beyond in their training regimen by not only giving a good work out but also a good understanding on how they work on and even why they work out like that". So a field earns its place by helping a trainer understand a client (how they train, and why), said as a sentence with its evidence, inside the rules above.

### Prior history

**Prior history is real history, and it comes from Mindbody, not FileMaker** (AJ, Oct 2 2026: "we need to be smart about this and stop hoping we get the filemaker data and start being realistic and going 'well lets just make the app work without it'", and "we dont need to know WHEN people joined journey. we just need to know how many sessions they have TOTAL and when was their first session"). **Total sessions = sessions before Journey + Journey's** (`src/lib/session-total.ts`, `sessionTotalOf`). Before Journey is GUESSED from Mindbody's visit count the sync already stores, less Journey's own sessions (`visitsBeforeJourney`), and a trainer CONFIRMS or corrects it once on Notes & Profile → Account ("About 306 before Journey (from Mindbody) · Confirm · Change"), which writes `client.priorHistory` with source `mindbody`. Until it is confirmed the Hub card, the header and the briefing show Mindbody's guess as her total ("#312") and the peek and the briefing say "from Mindbody, not yet confirmed"; milestones (and "first session") are claimed only off a confirmed total or a client whose whole story is in Journey (`canClaimMilestone`); with no guess at all a screen says "#6 in Journey". Late cancels never count as sessions. `client.priorHistory` records what a client did before the cutover (`src/lib/prior-history.ts`); `total = journey count + (sessions - importedCount)`. The reconciler owns what Journey can see, the prior record owns what it cannot, and they never write the same field. Any importer of historical sessions MUST raise `importedCount`. Never call a client new, draw a trend as their whole story, or say "never tried" off a low Journey count during migration. **Every sentence a screen says about a client's past** ("first time", "#N", a break, "All time") comes from `src/lib/history-claims.ts`, with coverage judged by the client's HOME studio's cutover (Sep 24 2026). "Client since" is `src/lib/client-since.ts` given that coverage, and the codex Story's since line is `storySince` (`client-story/story.ts`), on ONE rule (Oct 2 2026): **her first day is Mindbody's `firstAppointmentDate`**, shown "(from Mindbody)" until a trainer confirms or corrects it on Account → First day at the studio (which sets `firstStudioDay`); a client whose whole story is in Journey needs no confirming ("a new client's first Journey session sets it"). **Anniversaries wait for a confirmed date** (`canClaimAnniversary`; Operations → Month alone lists one by Mindbody's date, badged **Not confirmed** with where to confirm it — AJ, Oct 3 2026: "even if they are not confirmed we should still show them but signify that they are not confirmed"), and both count Journey's first session as her start only when Journey holds her whole story, so the two never disagree on one screen.

**Why the total leads, and why it can't come from FileMaker alone** (AJ, Oct 15 2026, a comment on the CLAUDE.md trim's review page): "When quickly looking at a client i really just want to know how many total sessions they have so i know what type of client to expect, are they new old or somewhere in the middle. but if i go deeper into the profile trying to understand their attendance it is nice to know how many sessions we have recorded for them vs how many they have done. ... currently if a client changed studios their history did not pull over and their new studio tracked them as a new client in filemaker but MindBody knew how many actual visits they had. some studios are even older than filemaker and probably even MindBody to an extent ... the main profile just has to show to total sessions but we also want to be able to learn more". So the header shows the total, the deeper pages show recorded beside done, and a trainer's Change on Account is the way to correct a total Mindbody undercounts for a client older than its records.

### First day at the studio

**A client's first day at the studio is set by a person, once, and every anniversary counts from it** (AJ, Sep 29 2026: "we need to make sure setting a client's anniversary for their first day in the studio is intuitive"). Every date the app could infer for when she started (her first Journey session, Mindbody's first visit, the day Mindbody made her record, her first package) is only an upper bound. `client.firstStudioDay` (`YYYY-MM-DD`) is set on Notes & Profile → Account → **First day at the studio** through the one record form; `lib/client-since.ts` reads it before every inferred date (source `stated`), so "Client since", the codex Story, the Directory, Today's and Week's milestones and Operations → Month all agree. Until it is set, Account says the app's guess and where it came from, and Month calls a guessed anniversary a guess. Nothing writes it for you.

**"Once" means nothing sets it automatically, never that it is locked** (AJ, Oct 15 2026: "as long as studio leaders can still edit that date just in case theres a error or issue with that date for some reason"). The date stays editable on Account after it is set, changed or cleared and kept by Save changes, by anyone who trains at or leads the client's home studio and by administrators (the `clients/{id}` update rule, `client-codex/access.ts` `canEdit`).

### The standing week

**The standing week is a check, never a booking** (AJ, Sep 27 2026). A trainer proposes their usual week (when they usually take clients, in up to three blocks a day since the Openings round, and their regulars) on My Profile, a studio leader agrees it on My Studio → Team, and Journey checks the coming week's Mindbody bookings against the agreed week and says where they differ — the free slots a leader can fill. It never writes a hold, a booking or availability to Mindbody: holds cost money, can email a client and would clash with the front desk, which books the regulars as recurring appointments. A day whose bookings weren't read is "can't tell", never "open". AJ's answers of Sep 27 (the voice review follow-up): an iPad offline, or a read only its cache answered, is "can't tell" too; a trainer's days **away** block out their slots (Journey's own record, no agreement needed: "if someone has a vacation then it should block it out"); colleagues see each other's AGREED week read-only ("schedules are open to all"; on My Studio → Openings → Who's usually in since the Openings round, because another trainer's profile can't be opened); a regular booked on the **studio rotation** ("{studio} Rotation") at her time counts as usual; an unbooked slot is simply open; and the check looks 7 days ahead ("14 can be loaded on the calendar view if needed").

### Mindbody freshness

**How fresh each Mindbody fact must be (AJ, Sep 26 2026 — the cost plan).** Today's and tomorrow's bookings: within about 30 minutes (the pull every 30 minutes is the guarantee; the webhook brings most changes in seconds). Days 3–30: once each morning. Packages and sessions remaining: when a sale happens (plus a fresh pull the morning of a session near the end of a package, at most weekly). Client details: when they change (the webhook, the profile's Sync). **Nothing syncs a studio's clients on a timer before its Journey cutover date** ("we don't randomly sync all of our clients and have a big Mindbody bill"): before it, `scripts/onboard-studio.ts`, at a pace AJ chooses. A new timer that asks Mindbody more often than this needs his OK. **Journey never books a Mindbody visit through the API** (Mindbody charges $2.50 an appointment booked; AJ, Oct 15 2026: "Never do is Book a mindbody visit because 2.50 fee is crazy for that"); until Oct 15 2026 it was a feature that needed his OK. How often a studio's schedule is pulled (`autoSyncEnabled`, `syncIntervalMinutes`) is administrators' alone (AJ, Sep 28 2026): Operations → Mindbody shows it to a studio leader read only, and the rules refuse anyone else. **One Mindbody read serves a whole site** (the Mindbody savings round, Oct 12 2026; AJ: "as low as possible without sacrificing data and information"): the schedule proxy shares a whole answer for two minutes (`src/lib/mindbody-appointment-cache.ts`; Operations → Mindbody's Pull the schedule now sends `fresh`), every studio on a site pulls at the same slot (`siteSlotOffsetMs` in `features/admin/syncPolicy.ts`), and the sync never lets an answer older than a booking's last write overwrite or cancel it (`rowIsNewerThanAnswer`). Each 30-minute check counts what it found that the webhook had not (`studios/{s}/sync/near-YYYY-MM-DD`): that count, not a guess, is what any slowing of the check waits on. `docs/rounds/2026-10-12-mindbody-savings.md`.

### Notes as threads

**A note is a thread, not a fact** (Notes round, Sep 20). Something opens, runs a while, and then closes or deepens; updates hang off the original rather than becoming new notes, so the whole story is in one place. The window, the loudness and `resolvedAt` live on the ROOT and nowhere else. **Contradicting a note ADDS to it** — a client who does the movement fine gets "performed overhead, seemed okay" on the thread, because the good outcome may have happened *because* of the note, and closing would delete the reason it went well. Any trainer may close a thread and any trainer may reopen it. **The three zones are derived, never set**: Open (a live or coming window, or an "always" note that shouts), Standing context (an "always" note at plain loudness), Resolved. One vocabulary, not two that drift. **Dismissal is per trainer, private, and undone by any update** — that is what keeps a quieter briefing from becoming a briefing that withholds, and nothing is ever hidden without a way back to it.

### Query indexes

**Every query ships with its index** (speed round, Oct 5 2026). Production Firestore is the Enterprise edition: it builds no index by itself, not even a single-field one, and a query without one still answers, by scanning the whole collection, billed by the byte, so nothing fails and nothing is slow in a test. A new query adds a composite of at least two fields to `indexes` in `firestore.indexes.json` (never `fieldOverrides`, which Enterprise refuses); `src/lib/firestore-indexes.test.ts` fails without it, and the ship script deploys indexes before anything else.

**Where it lives** (from `CLAUDE.md`'s map, as it stood on Oct 14 2026): `firestore.rules`; tests in `tests/firestore.rules.test.ts`; indexes in `firestore.indexes.json`, and **every query has its index**: `src/lib/firestore-indexes.test.ts` (over `src/lib/firestore-index-guard.ts`) reads every query in `src`, `server.ts`, `server/` and `functions/src` and prints the JSON to add for one without; the long explanations behind them are in `docs/ops/RULES-NOTES.md` (a comment of 8 lines or more goes there, and the lint holds the file under 240 KiB)

**Auto-renew is decided in one place** (`src/features/renewals/auto-renew.ts`, Sep 25 2026): no answer under a coach's paid-in-full or banked-sessions lock; else Mindbody's contract flag, else a trainer's mark on the profile for that contract (`client.autoRenewMark`), else the package's answer, else the studio's (My Studio → Studio → Renewals; default ON — franchise studios have it on, the corporate studios turn it off). A contract the package table doesn't recognise gets no studio default. Single-client screens read `renewalOf(client)`. `docs/rounds/2026-09-25-auto-renew.md` is the round.
